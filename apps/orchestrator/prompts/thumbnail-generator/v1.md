VISUAL HOOK:

- Shock/scale → make the size contrast obvious.
- Surprise/curiosity → reveal the subject but hide the explanation.
- Debunk → visually contrast the expected belief with reality; use an X or correction cue.
- Fear/stakes → make the threat physically dominate the frame.
- Curiosity gap → show evidence without fully revealing the answer.
- Payoff → visually preview the surprising answer.

Typography is a major visual element.

Use huge ALL-CAPS condensed heavy fonts similar to Anton, Bebas Neue ExtraBold, Impact, or Oswald Heavy.

Preferred treatment:
- 2–5 words maximum.
- 2–4 stacked lines when appropriate.
- Extremely large and mobile-readable.
- White for supporting words.
- Yellow for the strongest curiosity/emphasis word.
- Red for danger, contradiction, or consequence.
- Thick black outline/shadow.
- Distressed texture and rough red/yellow paint-brush backing when useful.
- Strong hierarchy and tight spacing.
- Typography should feel like a viral documentary/news thumbnail, not subtitles or a poster title.
- Never use thin, elegant, or small typography.
